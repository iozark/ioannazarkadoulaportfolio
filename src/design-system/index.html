Decision 1

Net profit as one number at the top of the dashboard

Decision

Lead with a single net-profit figure before charts or tables.

Alternatives considered

  • Line chart of income vs expenses
  • Full transaction table as the primary view

Trade-off

Less data density in exchange for an immediate answer to the user’s primary question.

Outcome

Faster comprehension of portfolio health in early usability sessions.

Insight

Participants asked for fewer charts and a clearer answer — not more analytics.
