About

Product thinking with technical depth

I am a Product Designer specializing in fintech and payment products, with 3+ years designing web and mobile applications for leading financial institutions. I own work from discovery and research through production-ready interfaces and developer handoff.

I collaborate closely with engineers and project managers to simplify complex financial workflows through accessible, scalable, and user-centered design. Clients and products I have contributed to include Worldline, Euronet, Cardlink, Nexi, EDPS, and NBG Payments.

I am currently pursuing a BSc (Hons) in Computer Science at Mediterranean College (University of Derby) to deepen my technical understanding of software development — so design decisions stay grounded in how products are actually built, secured, and shipped.
